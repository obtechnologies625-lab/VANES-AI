How to use VANES for the first time
  1. Type a question normally — VANES detects the likely subject.
  2. Use a white tag such as #analyze before your request.
  3. Tap the image button to analyse a photo of a question, notes, graph or diagram.
  4. Tap ✧ to request an AI-generated study image when your selected OpenRouter model supports image output.
