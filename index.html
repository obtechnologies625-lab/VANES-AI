VANES AI GUIDE

How to use VANES

Use VANES as your personal study assistant for the Tanzanian curriculum.

01
Ask your question

Type naturally in English or Kiswahili. VANES identifies the likely subject and education level, then explains the answer step by step.

02
Work with study images

Use the image upload button to send a question, notes, graph, diagram or other study material for analysis.

03
Choose what you need

Ask VANES to explain a topic, practise exam questions, analyse your work, make a study plan, summarise notes, translate text or mark an answer.

04
Generate a visual

Use ✧ and describe what you want. VANES creates an educational image.

05
Keep learning in one chat

Continue asking follow-up questions, use the chat history for previous conversations, and start a new chat whenever you change topics.

Study tip

For important exam work, compare answers with your teacher, textbook and the current official syllabus.
